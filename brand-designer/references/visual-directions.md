# Visual directions — structured concept presentations

Use after strategy and naming approvals and before the Manual de Marca. Present normally two or three **strategically distinct** directions, not merely color swaps. References inform craft and analysis; never copy third-party marks, layouts, artwork or trade dress.

## Research and inspiration sources

- Pentagram work: https://www.pentagram.com/work — examples of concept-to-system identity projects.
- COLLINS work: https://www.collins.studio/work — expressive identity systems and art direction.
- DesignStudio work: https://www.design.studio/work — integrated applications.
- Brand New: https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/ — critical identity analysis.
- Behance: https://www.behance.net/ — broad portfolio discovery; curate individual relevant case studies, do not rely on a general gallery alone.

Record the specific project/page, date, applicable insight and differentiation safeguards for any example actually consulted. These URLs are inspiration directories, not prescribed aesthetic templates or proof of rights.

## Deliverables for each direction

Create a **separate, inspectable, illustrated presentation** for each direction, typically 5–8 substantive pages when appropriate. Include: (1) central strategic idea and audience fit; (2) distinctive identity personality and verbal sample; (3) preliminary symbol/wordmark exploration; (4) semantic palette with proposed values; (5) typography specimens and sources; (6) visual grammar, illustration/photography direction; (7) two or more business-relevant application mockups; (8) tradeoffs, risks and production feasibility. Label every concept as proposed, not approved or production-ready.

Provide a concise side-by-side comparison using consistent criteria: coherence, distinctiveness, audience fit, legibility, scalability, practical production, accessibility and suitability for actual channels. Do not invent legal screening or market validation in artwork. Request explicit selection or revisions; only the selected direction proceeds to the manual draft.
