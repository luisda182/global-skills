# Theme 1 · Academic Clarity

ID: `academic-clarity` · version 1.0 · independent definition.
Purpose: student-facing assessments and study guides, digital and grayscale print from the same source.

## Visual tokens

| Role | Digital | Grayscale print |
|---|---|---|
| Page | White `#FFFFFF` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Main text and essential rules | Near-black `#202020` | Black `#000000` |
| Headings and underlined links | Deep blue `#174A67` | Black `#000000` |
| Secondary text, captions | Dark gray `#4A4A4A` | Dark gray `#333333` |
| Optional study box fill | Pale gray `#F2F2F2` | White with `#595959` border |
| Essential diagram strokes | `#595959` | `#333333` |

Only listed text/background pairs are intended: text on white or the pale gray box. No blue-on-gray color coding. Fill is decorative; borders and labels carry structure. Use white pages, no large dark banners or full-page fills. In assessments, use equal weight for all answer choices; no visual cues to correct responses.

## Typography and layout

Headings and body: Arial → Aptos → Calibri → Liberation Sans → an installed readable sans serif. Choose one family consistently; no font installation required. Body 12 pt, minimum 12 pt; title 20–24 pt; section headings 14–16 pt bold; captions/footers 10–11 pt, never use that smaller size for task-critical instructions. Body line spacing 1.15–1.3; space after paragraphs 6 pt. Use left alignment, restrained bold, no long all-capital paragraphs or justified body text.

New documents: A4 portrait, 20 mm margins, adjusted outward for protected letterhead. Preserve supplied size, margins, and required assessment pagination. Keep questions with their diagrams and enough answer space; do not compress answer lines to save paper. Use at least 0.75 pt essential rules. Tables use bold header text, explicit borders where needed, and repeated headers across pages; alternating fill is optional and never essential.

For an explicitly requested slide adaptation: retain the palette and font chain; titles 32–36 pt, body 24–28 pt, labels at least 18 pt; use light backgrounds and generous spacing. Preserve source aspect ratio, otherwise use 16:9.

## Grayscale compatibility

This theme is designed to work well in grayscale: hierarchy relies on size, weight, spacing, numbering and borders; all essential text is dark on a light background; no task depends on color. Digital blue becomes black in the print variant. The guarantee is conditional on content: graphs, imported scans and images must also pass inspection.

For every dual-use artifact render a grayscale proof at intended page size. Inspect all pages for lost distinctions, faint answer lines, cropped letterhead and merged chart series. Charts require direct labels plus distinct marker shapes/line styles or patterns. If a scientific task requires identifying colors (for example, an indicator experiment), preserve that learning objective and flag the need for a color insert or an authorized equivalent question; do not silently change the question. Do not call an unproofed output print-validated. Physical printer behavior remains unverified until a representative print is checked.
